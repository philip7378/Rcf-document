4.1 Global Time: The Non-Hermitian Arrow [E4]

    i rho_dot = [H_eff, rho] + i gamma sum_{b,q}

with exact norm-loss law d||psi||^2/dt = -gamma <M_hat> between
jumps. The one-way global arrow is carried algebraically by the

L_{b,q}^{cov3} = (J_{b,q} - Lambda_{b,q})/sqrt(2) (Paper I Eq. 4):

    sum_q (L_b^dagger L_b - L_b L_b^dagger)
        = P_b^{(2)} - (5/3) P_b^{(1)}   (machine: 4.4e-16 and
        2.2e-16 per bond, N = 4, gamma = 1; r5.1a Gate 0),

kernel K_N, the Jordan and Lie halves agree (J_q psi = Lambda_q psi
=> L_q psi = 0), freezing the reconciled past; off K_N, their
disagreement drives irreversible global drainage toward K_N. The
arrow is algebraic, not thermodynamic postulate: it is the fixed
sign of the projector mismatch above.
