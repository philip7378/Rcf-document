## Summary of Principal Changes from v2.1

### 1. Architectural Reordering
The Reconciliation Principle (RP) moves from a structural principle in §2 to the variational principle generated by the Lie–Jordan algebra in §0.7. Open Expansion is demoted from Postulate to Theorem (§1.2). Fracture, burden, and the bounded causal rate are derived consequences of RP rather than its prerequisites.

### 2. Two-Link Principle Derived (§0.5)
The Two-Link Principle (causal links = Lie, correlation links = Jordan, coupling via Leibniz) is no longer a primitive. It is a theorem of associativity (Lemma 0.6.1, with full proof in Appendix A.1).

### 3. F̂_MM Adopted as Primary (§0.7)
The framework's burden operator is now:
$$\hat{F}_{MM} = \sum_{\alpha,\beta} \omega(J_{\alpha\beta}^2) \cdot \pi(\Delta_{\alpha\beta})^\dagger \pi(\Delta_{\alpha\beta})$$
This bridges Lie and Jordan structure pair-by-pair. The original $\hat{F}$ is recovered in the uniform-Jordan limit (Proposition 0.7.1).

### 4. GAP-3.6.5 Closed (§3.6)
$D=3$ spatial dimensions are derived via:
- Two-Link derivation (§0.5) → Lie dynamics preserves SU(n) orbits (Lemma 3.6.1)
- Inflation-replacement requirement → full propagation via causal ancestry requires trivial orbit structure
- Transitivity theorem (Theorem 3.6.5): SU(n) acts transitively on the traceless sphere iff n=2
- Therefore $n=2$ → $\dim(\text{traceless } \mathfrak{su}(2)) = 3$

The $H_3(\mathbb{C})$ $d_{ijk}$ calculation (§3.7) is the load-bearing verification.

### 5. GAP-2.1.2 Closed (§0.7)
RP is derived as the variational principle of $\hat{F}_{MM}$. No new primitive is introduced.

### 6. Retractions Documented (§3.9, §7.8)
- Universal Geometry Lemma: retracted (Gram determinant is not algebra-blind)
- Layer-Closure Selector: retracted ($d_{ijk}$ is part of the Layer-L multiplication table)
- Hodge-Duality Argument: retracted (circular)
- Sector-dependent n picture: retracted (depends on Universal Geometry Lemma)

### 7. Open Research Directions (§7.8)
- Variance-form $B_{MM}$ and $n$-selection
- $B_{MM}^{(n)}$ variational problem
- RP minimization drives toward homogeneous states
- Connection to Standard Model gauge groups
